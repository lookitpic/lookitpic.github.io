A viewer for
WebP and AVIF
files

And not just those. It also opens JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO, HEIC, HEIF and SVG. It plays WebP and GIF animations, too. The new version also supports JPEG XL.

I felt like making a simple image viewer. Something easy to use, simple and lightweight, but with settings you could make your own. Then it somehow grew from there. More formats, a slideshow (of course) and printing found their way in.

It turned out quite nicely. Perhaps you’ll find it useful, too.

What it can do

Open a picture, zoom in (use the mouse wheel to adjust the zoom), view it full screen and browse the next one. Find the image you want in the thumbnail strip. Start a slideshow or print an image. With animated images, you can pause playback or change the speed.

If you feel like tinkering, you can customize the viewing background: choose a color, transparency, blur and saturation.

You can play with animation, too. Turn window animations on or off and adjust slideshow transitions. For the control bar, set the hide delay and fade duration.

And if you don’t need any of that, leave it as it is. It’s just an image viewer — for simply enjoying your photos :)
